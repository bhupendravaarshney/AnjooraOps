# Anjoora and AnjooraOps User Handbook

**Languages:** English and हिन्दी  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Updated:** 8 September 2026  
**For:** Anjoora customers, Vaidyas, support staff, operations staff, and administrators

This handbook explains the two applications in everyday language:

| Application | Used by | Main purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Anjoora** | Customers | Complete a wellness questionnaire, send a folio for human review, and continue on WhatsApp. |
| **AnjooraOps** | Authorized staff | Review consultations, manage WhatsApp conversations, recommendations, orders, stock, preparation, dispatch, refills, and privacy requests. |

> **Important safety note:** Anjoora is a wellness service. It does not diagnose illness, replace a doctor, change prescribed medicines, or provide emergency care. If the screen tells you to stop and seek urgent help, do that first. In India, the national emergency number is **112**; elsewhere, use your local emergency number.

# English user guide

## Part A — Anjoora customer guide

### 1. Before you begin

Keep these things ready:

- A phone with WhatsApp.
- About 5–10 minutes.
- Honest information about your main concern, daily routine, medicines, allergies, pregnancy, age, and any worrying symptoms.
- A private device if possible. Your unfinished answers are saved only in that browser on that device.

The local demonstration address is `http://localhost:3001`. Your organization may provide a different production web address.

### 2. Start your wellness folio

1. Open Anjoora and select **Begin my consultation**.
2. Choose between **one and four concerns** that you want the Vaidya to consider.
3. Mark one selected concern as your **primary concern**. This is the issue that matters most right now.
4. Answer the six short groups of questions:
   - **Concerns:** what you want help with.
   - **Present pattern:** your goal, how long it has been happening, and its effect on daily life.
   - **Body tendencies:** appetite/digestion, body temperature tendency, and energy pattern.
   - **Daily rhythm:** food routine, sleep routine, and realistic habits.
   - **Inner climate:** stress response, emotional support, and preferred style of change.
   - **Preparation:** preferred format and the safety check.
5. Use **Back** if you want to change an earlier answer. Use **Continue** only after answering every question shown on the screen.
6. Select **Prepare my apothecary brief** to see the summary.

Your selected concerns stay together in one folio. The primary concern is shown first; the other concerns remain linked so they are not lost.

### 3. Understand the safety result

Answer every safety question honestly. Selecting “none” clears other safety choices; do not choose “none” if another item applies.

| Result | What it means | What you should do |
|---|---|---|
| **Clear** | None of the listed safety items was selected. | Continue to human review. This is not a medical clearance or diagnosis. |
| **Human review required** | A medicine, allergy, pregnancy, child-age case, or similar context needs attention. | Submit the folio and wait for the Vaidya. Do not stop prescribed medicine unless your treating clinician tells you to. |
| **Urgent stop** | Your answer may need prompt medical assessment. | Stop the wellness flow and seek urgent medical help. No Anjoora recommendation should be prepared for that symptom. |

If your condition changes or becomes severe after submission, seek appropriate medical help instead of waiting for WhatsApp.

### 4. Send the folio for Vaidya review

1. On the summary screen, read your main and linked concerns.
2. Enter your name and mobile number.
3. Read the consent sentence and tick it only if you agree.
4. Select **Send my brief for Vaidya review**.
5. On the connection screen, confirm your full name and WhatsApp number.
6. Enter your city, preferred language, and best time for a message.
7. Read and accept the contact consent.
8. Select **Send my folio for Vaidya review**.

Anjoora first saves the folio in AnjooraOps. WhatsApp opens only after the save succeeds. Note the folio number shown on the confirmation screen; it is useful when asking for help.

When WhatsApp opens, review the prepared message and press **Send** inside WhatsApp. Opening WhatsApp alone does not send the message.

If WhatsApp does not open but the folio was saved, use **Open WhatsApp again**. Do not repeatedly create a new consultation.

### 5. What happens after submission

The normal journey is:

1. A Vaidya reads the full folio and safety answers.
2. The team may ask a clarification question on WhatsApp.
3. You answer in the same WhatsApp conversation.
4. The Vaidya prepares a human-reviewed recommendation, or explains why the case should be paused/closed.
5. If a recommendation is suitable, the team sends a clear order-review link.
6. You review and accept or ask for a change.
7. Payment is requested only after acceptance.
8. After confirmed payment, the standard product is allocated or a personalized preparation is made.
9. The team shares dispatch/delivery information.
10. Near the planned follow-up date, the team may ask whether you want the same plan, a review/change, or to stop.

WhatsApp is the conversation channel. AnjooraOps is the official record of the consultation, order, payment, dispatch, and refill status.

### 6. Review and accept an order

When you receive an order-review link:

1. Check that the order number and your name look correct.
2. Read the recommendation/item description.
3. Check the subtotal, discount, tax, shipping charge, and total.
4. If anything is wrong, do **not** accept. Ask the team to correct it.
5. If everything is correct, tick the acceptance box and select **Accept order**.

Acceptance and payment are separate steps. Accepting says that you agree with the shown recommendation and total; it does not mean money has been collected.

### 7. Pay safely

- Use only the secure payment link sent through the official Anjoora conversation.
- Check the amount before approving payment.
- Never share your card PIN, UPI PIN, password, or OTP with Anjoora staff.
- Do not pay a different amount requested through an unknown number or personal account.
- If a payment is debited but the order is not updated, send the order number and provider transaction reference to support. Do not send card or PIN details.
- Preparation or stock allocation begins only after payment is confirmed in AnjooraOps.

### 8. Dispatch, delivery, and refill

- **Ready to dispatch:** the paid item has been allocated or prepared.
- **Dispatched/Shipped:** the courier and tracking/AWB details have been recorded.
- **Delivered:** staff have confirmed delivery.
- **Refill due:** the planned duration is nearing its end.

For a refill, tell the team one of the following:

- **Same:** you want the same approved plan again.
- **Modify:** you want a change or another Vaidya review.
- **Stop:** you do not want another order.

If you developed a new symptom, side effect, allergy, became pregnant, started a new medicine, or your clinician changed treatment, tell the team before choosing **Same**.

### 9. Correct, access, or remove your information

Contact the official support channel and ask for:

- **Access:** a copy of the information held about you.
- **Correction:** correction of inaccurate basic details.
- **Anonymization:** removal or masking of identifying information where allowed.

The team must verify your identity before processing a privacy request. Some information may need to be retained when a legal hold or other lawful requirement applies.

### 10. Install Anjoora on a phone

The website must be on HTTPS for normal phone installation.

**Android (Chrome)**

1. Open the Anjoora website in Chrome.
2. Select **Install** when shown.
3. Approve the browser prompt.

**iPhone or iPad (Safari)**

1. Open the Anjoora website in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

Your saved questionnaire progress remains tied to that browser/device. It is not a customer account and does not automatically move to another phone.

### 11. Customer troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Continue** is not working | Look for the highlighted unanswered question and choose an answer. |
| You cannot select a fifth concern | The maximum is four. Remove one before selecting another. |
| The wrong concern is primary | Use the primary-concern choice on the first screen and select the correct one. |
| Old answers appeared | The browser restored an unfinished local draft. Review the answers or restart the consultation. |
| Answers disappeared | Drafts exist only on the same browser/device. Private mode, clearing site data, or changing device can remove them. |
| Phone number is rejected | Enter a real reachable number, preferably with country code, such as `+91` for India. |
| Folio cannot be saved | Check the internet connection and try once more. If it continues, contact support; WhatsApp will not open until saving succeeds. |
| WhatsApp did not open | Allow the browser to open WhatsApp, or use **Open WhatsApp again** on the saved confirmation page. |
| You submitted twice | Give support the folio number. The system safely reuses an identical retry, but a deliberately new submission may create a new folio. |
| Payment succeeded but status did not change | Contact support with the order number and payment transaction reference. Never share your PIN or OTP. |
| The order link expired or does not open | Ask the official support team to verify the order and send the approved next step. |

### 12. Customer do and do not list

**Do**

- Give accurate safety information.
- Keep your folio and order numbers for reference.
- Review the total before accepting or paying.
- Use the same official WhatsApp thread.
- Tell the team about medicines, pregnancy, allergies, or changed symptoms.

**Do not**

- Use Anjoora for an emergency.
- Stop prescribed treatment based only on the website.
- Share an OTP, PIN, password, or full payment credential.
- Accept an order whose description or amount is wrong.
- Assume a website wellness summary is a diagnosis.

## Part B — AnjooraOps staff guide

### 1. Who should use AnjooraOps

Only authorized staff may sign in. Always use your own named account.

| Role | Main responsibilities | Screens normally available |
|---|---|---|
| **ADMIN** | Security oversight, all operational work, privacy requests, and verified manual payment reconciliation. | All screens. |
| **VAIDYA** | Safety/consultation review, clarification, recommendations, and clinical WhatsApp support. | Dashboard, Consultations, WhatsApp, account security. |
| **OPERATIONS** | Orders, stock, personalized batches, dispatch, delivery, and refills. | Dashboard, Consultations, Orders, Inventory, Batches, Dispatch, Refills, account security. |
| **SUPPORT** | Human WhatsApp conversations and customer coordination. | Dashboard, WhatsApp, account security. |

If you cannot see a screen or action, first check whether it belongs to your role. Do not borrow another person's account.

Administrators create named users from **Staff**. Enter the person's full name, named work email, role, and a temporary password. New users must change that password on first sign-in. The SUPPORT role can use only Dashboard and WhatsApp operational tabs; hiding the other tabs is backed by server-side permission checks.

The local staff login is `http://localhost:3000/admin/login` after the system owner starts the services. Production may use another secure address.

### 2. Sign in and secure your account

1. Open the staff login page.
2. Enter your work email and password.
3. Select **Sign in**.

After repeated wrong attempts, the account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait for the lock to end or follow your organization's support procedure; do not keep guessing.

**First sign-in/password change**

1. If asked, enter the current temporary password.
2. Create a password of 12–128 characters with uppercase, lowercase, number, and symbol.
3. Confirm it and select **Change password and sign out all sessions**.
4. Sign in again with the new password.

**Review sessions**

1. Select your name/role in the top bar.
2. On **Active sessions**, review created, last-seen, and expiry times.
3. Select **Revoke** for a session you do not recognize.
4. Change your password immediately if you suspect account misuse.

Always select **Logout** when you finish, especially on a shared workstation.

### 3. Use the dashboard

The dashboard is different for each role. It may show:

- consultations needing Vaidya attention;
- active orders;
- open personalized batches;
- refills due soon;
- WhatsApp conversations needing a person;
- the latest consultations and safety result.

Open a count or use the top navigation to work in the relevant queue. A missing queue usually means your role does not have that permission.

### 4. Find records

Most lists have a search box. Search with the information named in the placeholder, such as customer name, phone, folio, order number, batch number, SKU, AWB, refill number, or privacy-request number.

- Use **Clear** to remove a search.
- Use **Next page** or **Older messages** instead of assuming the first page contains every record.
- Prefer public record numbers such as `ANJ-FOL-...` or `ANJ-ORD-...` when discussing a case internally.
- Confirm the customer and record number before changing anything.

### 5. Vaidya consultation workflow

1. Open **Consultations**.
2. Search by name, phone, or folio; use concern and safety filters when needed.
3. Open the consultation.
4. Read the primary concern, every linked concern, the complete folio, safety panel, and later WhatsApp notes.
5. Act on the safety result first:
   - **URGENT STOP:** do not create a wellness recommendation. Follow the approved urgent-referral procedure and hold/close the case as appropriate.
   - **REVIEW REQUIRED:** clarify medicines, allergies, pregnancy, age, or other flagged context before proceeding.
   - **CLEAR:** continue professional review; “clear” is not a diagnosis.
6. Choose one review action:
   - **Request clarification:** write one clear question for the customer.
   - **Ready for recommendation:** use only when review is complete and no urgent block exists.
   - **Hold:** pause the case without approving it.
   - **Close:** end the case when no further workflow should continue.
7. When the case is ready, create the recommendation:
   - write a plain summary and usage instructions;
   - choose **Standard product** or **Personalised formula**;
   - enter a positive duration in days;
   - for a standard item, enter its product SKU/public ID;
   - for a personalized formula, enter its name and format, select every ingredient from the catalogue dropdown, and enter each quantity. SKU, name, unit, and inventory mapping are resolved from the database.
8. Check the information, then select **Approve recommendation**.
9. If a valid revision is needed, use **Create revised recommendation / next formula version**. Do not alter the historical customer submission.

Only a Vaidya or administrator should make review/recommendation decisions. Automation and operations staff must not make clinical choices.

### 6. Support and Vaidya WhatsApp workflow

1. Open **WhatsApp**.
2. Search by customer name or phone.
3. Select the correct conversation and confirm its folio/order context.
4. Read the latest messages, intent, delivery status, and any error. Use **Older messages** when required.
5. If the conversation shows **HUMAN SUPPORT**, read the full context before replying.
6. Type a clear reply in **Human reply** and select **Queue through WhatsApp** once.
7. The message is sent by a background worker. Check whether it becomes sent, delivered, read, or failed; do not repeatedly click the button.
8. If delivery fails, follow the messaging escalation procedure. Do not copy customer health/payment data into an unapproved personal messaging channel.

Support staff can coordinate and answer approved service questions, but must pass recommendation, medicine, symptom, pregnancy, allergy, or urgent-safety decisions to a Vaidya.

### 7. Create an order after recommendation

An order can be created only for the current approved recommendation.

1. Open the consultation that contains the approved recommendation.
2. In the order section, enter:
   - subtotal;
   - discount, if approved;
   - tax;
   - shipping charge;
   - shipping address.
3. Recheck the calculated commercial details.
4. Select **Create order for customer acceptance** once.
5. The system creates one order and queues the customer's secure acceptance link.

Do not create another order because the customer has not replied. Search the **Orders** page and check the existing order first.

### 8. Acceptance and payment

On **Orders**, watch both the order and payment status.

- **Awaiting acceptance:** the customer has not accepted the shown order.
- **Awaiting payment / Payment pending:** acceptance is recorded but payment is not confirmed.
- **Payment review required:** the provider event needs authorized investigation.
- **Paid:** fulfillment may begin.
- **Failed/Cancelled/Refunded:** do not fulfill unless a new valid paid state is created through the approved procedure.

Only an administrator may use **Reconcile paid**, and only after independently verifying the provider/bank evidence. Enter the verified reference; never use manual reconciliation just to unblock an order.

### 9. Inventory workflow

**Add or update an item**

1. Open **Inventory**.
2. Enter SKU, name, canonical unit (`g`, `kg`, `ml`, `l`, or `unit`), and reorder level.
3. Select **Save item**.

Use one SKU for one real item. A unit cannot be casually changed after stock transactions exist.

**Receive or correct stock**

1. Enter the item SKU/public ID.
2. Choose **Receipt (+)** for stock physically received.
3. Choose **Adjustment (+/−)** only for an approved correction based on a physical check.
4. Enter quantity, reference, and a useful non-sensitive note.
5. Select **Post immutable transaction**.

Transactions cannot be edited or deleted. Correct a mistake with a new opposite/adjusting transaction and an explanation.

**Map a standard product**

1. In **Finished-goods mapping**, enter the product SKU/public ID.
2. Enter the inventory item SKU/public ID.
3. Enter the quantity consumed for one ordered unit.
4. Save the mapping.

A paid standard-product order cannot be allocated until this mapping and enough stock exist.

**Manage formula ingredients**

1. Create the raw-material inventory item first and confirm its canonical unit.
2. In **Formula ingredient catalogue**, select that inventory item and optionally set the practitioner-facing display name.
3. Select **Add/update ingredient**. Only active catalogue entries appear in recommendation dropdowns.
4. Deactivate an entry to prevent new selection. Existing formula history keeps its recorded snapshot and is not rewritten.

### 10. Fulfill a standard-product order

1. Confirm the order status is **PAID**.
2. Confirm the product-to-inventory mapping and available stock.
3. On **Orders**, select **Allocate stock → dispatch** once.
4. The system records the stock issue and moves the order to **READY TO DISPATCH**.

If stock is insufficient or unmapped, fix the real stock/mapping issue. Never post a false adjustment just to bypass the check.

### 11. Fulfill a personalized order

1. Confirm the personalized order status is **PAID**.
2. Select **Prepare batch**, or open **Batches**.
3. Enter the paid order public ID and approved wastage percentage.
4. Select **Create order-sized requirements**.
5. Check every ingredient, quantity, and unit against the approved formula and physical preparation record.
6. When preparation is genuinely complete, select **Complete and consume stock** once.
7. The system locks/consumes the required stock and makes the order ready for dispatch.

If an ingredient is missing, unmapped, in a different unit, or short on stock, stop and correct the underlying issue. Do not substitute an ingredient without a new Vaidya-approved formula revision.

### 12. Dispatch and delivery

1. Open **Dispatch** and find the order.
2. For an order that is **READY TO DISPATCH**, enter the courier and AWB/tracking number.
3. Confirm the package and details, then select **Mark dispatched**.
4. Select **Mark delivered** only after reliable delivery confirmation.

Marking delivery creates the refill schedule from the full recommendation duration. A mistaken delivery action should be escalated; do not try to repair history through direct database changes.

### 13. Refill workflow

Open **Refills** and check the due date, last reminder, current status, and source order.

- Select **Same** only when the customer wants the same plan and no new review is required. It creates at most one next order.
- Select **Modify** when symptoms, preferences, safety context, medicine, or plan need a new Vaidya review.
- Select **Stop** when the customer does not want to continue.

Read the conversation before deciding. If the customer mentions a new safety concern, send the case for Vaidya review instead of selecting **Same**.

### 14. Administrator privacy workflow

Only an administrator may use **Privacy**.

1. Verify the requester's identity using the approved procedure outside the form.
2. Open **Privacy** and enter the customer public ID or exact phone.
3. Choose `ACCESS`, `CORRECTION`, or `ANONYMIZATION`.
4. Tick the identity-verification confirmation.
5. Add only a short, non-sensitive verification note.
6. Select **Record request**.

Then:

- For **ACCESS**, use **Export** only for the verified requester and deliver it through the approved secure method.
- For **CORRECTION**, enter only the corrected fields and complete the request.
- For **ANONYMIZATION**, confirm approval and consequences before completion. This cannot be treated like a reversible edit.
- Use **Apply legal hold** only with an approved reason. While held, anonymization and automated deletion are blocked.
- Use **Release legal hold** only when authorized, and record the reason.

Never place identity documents, passwords, medical detail, or payment credentials in the verification-note field.

### 15. Common staff messages and errors

| Message or situation | Meaning and safe response |
|---|---|
| **401 / Authentication required** | Sign in again. Your session may have expired or been revoked. |
| **403 / No permission** | Your role does not allow the action, password rotation is required, or the request did not come from the approved page. Do not use another account. |
| **409 / State conflict** | Someone or another process already changed the record. Reload it, read the current status, and decide again. |
| **422 / Validation error** | A field is missing, invalid, too long, or uses an unsupported value/unit. Correct the input rather than retrying unchanged. |
| Action button is missing | The current role or record status does not allow that action. Check prerequisites. |
| Insufficient stock | Check physical stock, receipts/adjustments, mapping, unit, order quantity, and batch wastage. |
| WhatsApp message is pending | The outbox worker has not accepted/sent it yet. Wait and check status; escalate if delayed. |
| WhatsApp message failed | Read the error and follow the messaging incident procedure. Do not duplicate the message repeatedly. |
| Anonymization is blocked | Check for a legal hold and obtain authorized direction. |
| Login is locked | Wait 15 minutes and follow the account-support procedure if necessary. |

### 16. Staff do and do not list

**Do**

- Confirm customer and public record number before every action.
- Read all concerns and safety flags, not only the first concern.
- Respect role boundaries and obtain required approval.
- Use factual, non-sensitive audit/reference notes.
- Reload a record after a conflict.
- Logout and revoke unknown sessions.

**Do not**

- Share accounts, passwords, or provider secrets.
- Bypass payment, stock, safety, or workflow status checks.
- Mark payment, production, dispatch, or delivery without real evidence.
- Edit database records directly to correct an operational mistake.
- Put unnecessary personal, medical, or payment data in notes.
- Promise a medical result or allow automation to approve a recommendation.

# हिन्दी उपयोगकर्ता मार्गदर्शिका

## भाग A — Anjoora ग्राहक मार्गदर्शिका

### 1. शुरू करने से पहले

ये चीज़ें तैयार रखें:

- ऐसा मोबाइल जिसमें WhatsApp चलता हो।
- लगभग 5–10 मिनट का समय।
- अपनी मुख्य परेशानी, दिनचर्या, दवाइयों, एलर्जी, गर्भावस्था, उम्र और किसी गंभीर लक्षण की सही जानकारी।
- संभव हो तो निजी मोबाइल/कंप्यूटर। अधूरे उत्तर केवल उसी डिवाइस के उसी ब्राउज़र में सेव होते हैं।

लोकल डेमो का पता `http://localhost:3001` है। असली सेवा का वेब पता संस्था अलग से दे सकती है।

### 2. अपना wellness folio शुरू करें

1. Anjoora खोलें और **Begin my consultation** चुनें।
2. अपनी **एक से चार concerns (परेशानियाँ/लक्ष्य)** चुनें।
3. चुनी हुई concerns में से एक को **primary concern (मुख्य परेशानी)** बनाएं। यह वह बात है जो अभी आपके लिए सबसे महत्वपूर्ण है।
4. छह छोटे भागों के प्रश्न पूरे करें:
   - **Concerns:** आप किस बात के लिए सहायता चाहते हैं।
   - **Present pattern:** आपका लक्ष्य, परेशानी कितने समय से है और रोज़मर्रा पर उसका असर।
   - **Body tendencies:** भूख/पाचन, शरीर में गर्मी-ठंडक और ऊर्जा का ढंग।
   - **Daily rhythm:** भोजन, नींद और वे आदतें जिन्हें आप सच में अपना सकते हैं।
   - **Inner climate:** तनाव में आपकी प्रतिक्रिया, भावनात्मक जरूरत और बदलाव का पसंदीदा तरीका।
   - **Preparation:** पसंदीदा रूप और safety check (सुरक्षा जांच)।
5. पिछला उत्तर बदलने के लिए **Back** दबाएं। स्क्रीन के सभी जरूरी उत्तर भरने के बाद ही **Continue** दबाएं।
6. सारांश देखने के लिए **Prepare my apothecary brief** चुनें।

आपकी सभी चुनी concerns एक ही folio में रहती हैं। मुख्य concern पहले दिखती है और बाकी concerns उससे जुड़ी रहती हैं; वे खोती नहीं हैं।

### 3. Safety result को समझें

सुरक्षा से जुड़े सभी उत्तर सच-सच दें। यदि कोई और safety item लागू है, तो “none” न चुनें। “none” चुनने पर दूसरी safety choices हट जाती हैं।

| परिणाम | इसका मतलब | आपको क्या करना है |
|---|---|---|
| **Clear** | सूची में दी गई कोई safety condition नहीं चुनी गई। | Human review के लिए आगे बढ़ें। इसका मतलब medical clearance या diagnosis नहीं है। |
| **Human review required** | दवा, एलर्जी, गर्भावस्था, कम उम्र या ऐसी ही जानकारी को Vaidya को देखना जरूरी है। | Folio भेजें और Vaidya के उत्तर का इंतज़ार करें। डॉक्टर की सलाह के बिना prescribed medicine बंद न करें। |
| **Urgent stop** | उत्तर किसी ऐसी स्थिति की ओर इशारा करता है जिसमें जल्दी medical assessment जरूरी हो सकता है। | Wellness प्रक्रिया रोकें और तुरंत उचित चिकित्सा सहायता लें। इस लक्षण के लिए Anjoora recommendation नहीं बननी चाहिए। |

यदि folio भेजने के बाद हालत बिगड़ती है या लक्षण गंभीर हो जाते हैं, तो WhatsApp के उत्तर का इंतज़ार न करें; उचित चिकित्सा सहायता लें। भारत में emergency number **112** है।

### 4. Folio को Vaidya review के लिए भेजें

1. Summary screen पर अपनी मुख्य और जुड़ी concerns पढ़ें।
2. अपना नाम और मोबाइल नंबर भरें।
3. Consent (सहमति) का वाक्य पढ़ें और सहमत होने पर ही tick करें।
4. **Send my brief for Vaidya review** दबाएं।
5. अगले connection screen पर पूरा नाम और WhatsApp नंबर जांचें।
6. अपना शहर, पसंदीदा भाषा और message का सही समय चुनें।
7. Contact consent पढ़कर स्वीकार करें।
8. **Send my folio for Vaidya review** दबाएं।

Anjoora पहले folio को AnjooraOps में सुरक्षित करता है। Save सफल होने के बाद ही WhatsApp खुलता है। Confirmation screen पर दिखा folio number नोट कर लें; सहायता मांगते समय यह काम आएगा।

WhatsApp खुलने पर तैयार message पढ़ें और WhatsApp के अंदर **Send** दबाएं। केवल WhatsApp खुल जाने से message नहीं जाता।

यदि folio save हो गया लेकिन WhatsApp नहीं खुला, तो **Open WhatsApp again** चुनें। बार-बार नई consultation न बनाएं।

### 5. Folio भेजने के बाद क्या होता है

सामान्य प्रक्रिया इस प्रकार है:

1. Vaidya पूरा folio और safety answers पढ़ता है।
2. जरूरत होने पर टीम WhatsApp पर clarification (और जानकारी) मांगती है।
3. आप उसी WhatsApp conversation में उत्तर देते हैं।
4. Vaidya human-reviewed recommendation बनाता है, या बताता है कि case को रोकना/बंद करना क्यों जरूरी है।
5. उचित recommendation होने पर order review link भेजा जाता है।
6. आप विवरण देखकर accept करते हैं या बदलाव मांगते हैं।
7. Acceptance के बाद ही payment मांगा जाता है।
8. Payment confirm होने पर standard product allocate होता है या personalized preparation बनती है।
9. टीम dispatch और delivery की जानकारी देती है।
10. Plan की अवधि पूरी होने के पास टीम पूछ सकती है: वही plan चाहिए, बदलाव/review चाहिए, या बंद करना है।

WhatsApp बातचीत का माध्यम है। Consultation, order, payment, dispatch और refill का आधिकारिक record AnjooraOps में रहता है।

### 6. Order देखें और स्वीकार करें

Order review link मिलने पर:

1. Order number और अपना नाम जांचें।
2. Recommendation/item का विवरण पढ़ें।
3. Subtotal, discount, tax, shipping और total देखें।
4. कुछ गलत हो तो accept न करें; टीम से correction मांगें।
5. सब सही हो तो acceptance box tick करके **Accept order** दबाएं।

Order accept करना और payment करना अलग-अलग steps हैं। Accept करने का मतलब है कि आप दिखाई गई recommendation और total से सहमत हैं; इससे अपने-आप payment नहीं होता।

### 7. सुरक्षित payment करें

- केवल official Anjoora conversation से आया secure payment link इस्तेमाल करें।
- Payment approve करने से पहले amount जांचें।
- अपना card PIN, UPI PIN, password या OTP किसी Anjoora staff को न बताएं।
- किसी अनजान नंबर या personal account पर अलग amount न भेजें।
- पैसा कट गया लेकिन order update नहीं हुआ, तो support को order number और provider transaction reference दें। Card/PIN की जानकारी न दें।
- Payment AnjooraOps में confirm होने के बाद ही तैयारी या stock allocation शुरू होना चाहिए।

### 8. Dispatch, delivery और refill

- **Ready to dispatch:** paid item तैयार या allocate हो चुका है।
- **Dispatched/Shipped:** courier और tracking/AWB दर्ज हो चुका है।
- **Delivered:** staff ने delivery confirm कर दी है।
- **Refill due:** plan की तय अवधि समाप्त होने वाली है।

Refill पर टीम को इनमें से एक उत्तर दें:

- **Same:** वही approved plan फिर चाहिए।
- **Modify:** बदलाव या नया Vaidya review चाहिए।
- **Stop:** अगला order नहीं चाहिए।

नया symptom, side effect, allergy, pregnancy, नई medicine या doctor द्वारा treatment change होने पर **Same** चुनने से पहले टीम को जरूर बताएं।

### 9. अपनी जानकारी की copy, correction या anonymization

Official support channel पर संपर्क करके इनमें से request करें:

- **Access:** आपके बारे में रखी जानकारी की copy।
- **Correction:** गलत basic details को सही करना।
- **Anonymization:** जहां अनुमति हो, पहचान बताने वाली जानकारी हटाना या छिपाना।

Privacy request पूरी करने से पहले टीम आपकी पहचान verify करेगी। Legal hold या कानूनी जरूरत होने पर कुछ जानकारी रखना जरूरी हो सकता है।

### 10. मोबाइल पर Anjoora install करें

सामान्य phone installation के लिए website का HTTPS पर होना जरूरी है।

**Android (Chrome)**

1. Chrome में Anjoora website खोलें।
2. **Install** चुनें।
3. Browser का installation prompt स्वीकार करें।

**iPhone/iPad (Safari)**

1. Safari में Anjoora website खोलें।
2. **Share** दबाएं।
3. **Add to Home Screen**, फिर **Add** चुनें।

Questionnaire की saved progress उसी browser/device से जुड़ी रहती है। यह customer account नहीं है और अपने-आप दूसरे phone में नहीं आती।

## भाग B — AnjooraOps staff मार्गदर्शिका

### 1. AnjooraOps कौन इस्तेमाल करे

केवल authorized staff login करे। हमेशा अपना named account इस्तेमाल करें।

| Role | मुख्य जिम्मेदारी | आम तौर पर उपलब्ध screens |
|---|---|---|
| **ADMIN** | Security देखना, सभी operations, privacy requests और verified manual payment reconciliation। | सभी screens। |
| **VAIDYA** | Safety/consultation review, clarification, recommendation और clinical WhatsApp support। | Dashboard, Consultations, WhatsApp और account security। |
| **OPERATIONS** | Orders, stock, personalized batches, dispatch, delivery और refills। | Dashboard, Consultations, Orders, Inventory, Batches, Dispatch, Refills और account security। |
| **SUPPORT** | Human WhatsApp conversation और customer coordination। | Dashboard, WhatsApp और account security। |

यदि कोई screen/action नहीं दिख रही, तो पहले अपना role देखें। किसी दूसरे व्यक्ति का account इस्तेमाल न करें।

Administrator **Staff** tab से named user बनाता है। Full name, named work email, role और temporary password भरें। नया user पहले login पर password बदलेगा। SUPPORT role केवल Dashboard और WhatsApp operational tabs इस्तेमाल कर सकता है; server APIs भी बाकी access रोकती हैं।

System owner द्वारा services शुरू करने के बाद local staff login `http://localhost:3000/admin/login` है। Production में अलग secure address हो सकता है।

### 2. Login और account security

1. Staff login page खोलें।
2. Work email और password भरें।
3. **Sign in** दबाएं।

बार-बार गलत login करने पर account 15 मिनट के लिए lock होता है। समय पूरा होने दें या organization की support प्रक्रिया अपनाएं; password guess करते न रहें।

**पहला login/password change**

1. मांगा जाए तो current temporary password भरें।
2. 12–128 characters का नया password बनाएं जिसमें uppercase, lowercase, number और symbol हों।
3. Password दोबारा भरकर **Change password and sign out all sessions** दबाएं।
4. नए password से फिर login करें।

**Active sessions देखें**

1. ऊपर अपना name/role चुनें।
2. **Active sessions** में created, last seen और expiry time देखें।
3. अनजान session पर **Revoke** दबाएं।
4. Account misuse का शक हो तो तुरंत password बदलें।

काम खत्म होने पर हमेशा **Logout** करें, खासकर shared computer पर।

### 3. Dashboard समझें

हर role का dashboard अलग हो सकता है। इसमें ये queues दिख सकती हैं:

- Vaidya attention वाली consultations;
- active orders;
- खुले personalized batches;
- जल्दी due होने वाले refills;
- human reply चाहने वाली WhatsApp conversations;
- latest consultations और safety result।

संबंधित काम के लिए ऊपर का navigation इस्तेमाल करें। Queue न दिखना अक्सर role permission का परिणाम होता है।

### 4. Record खोजें

अधिकतर lists में search box है। Placeholder के अनुसार customer name, phone, folio, order number, batch number, SKU, AWB, refill number या privacy request number से search करें।

- Search हटाने के लिए **Clear** दबाएं।
- बाकी records देखने के लिए **Next page** या **Older messages** इस्तेमाल करें।
- Internal बातचीत में `ANJ-FOL-...` या `ANJ-ORD-...` जैसा public number इस्तेमाल करें।
- कोई बदलाव करने से पहले customer और record number दोनों verify करें।

### 5. Vaidya consultation workflow

1. **Consultations** खोलें।
2. Name, phone या folio से search करें; जरूरत पर concern और safety filter लगाएं।
3. सही consultation खोलें।
4. Primary concern, सभी linked concerns, पूरा folio, safety panel और WhatsApp notes पढ़ें।
5. सबसे पहले safety result पर काम करें:
   - **URGENT STOP:** wellness recommendation न बनाएं। Approved urgent-referral process अपनाएं और case को उचित रूप से hold/close करें।
   - **REVIEW REQUIRED:** medicine, allergy, pregnancy, age या दूसरे flag को clarify करें।
   - **CLEAR:** professional review जारी रखें; “clear” diagnosis नहीं है।
6. एक review action चुनें:
   - **Request clarification:** customer के लिए एक साफ प्रश्न लिखें।
   - **Ready for recommendation:** review पूरा होने और urgent block न होने पर ही चुनें।
   - **Hold:** case को बिना approval के रोकें।
   - **Close:** आगे workflow नहीं चलना हो तो case बंद करें।
7. Case ready हो तो recommendation बनाएं:
   - आसान summary और usage instructions लिखें;
   - **Standard product** या **Personalised formula** चुनें;
   - positive duration days भरें;
   - standard item के लिए product SKU/public ID भरें;
   - personalized formula के लिए name और format भरें, catalogue dropdown से हर ingredient चुनें और quantity डालें। SKU, name, unit और inventory mapping database से आते हैं।
8. सब जांचकर **Approve recommendation** दबाएं।
9. सही revision की जरूरत हो तो **Create revised recommendation / next formula version** इस्तेमाल करें। Customer का original submission न बदलें।

Review और recommendation decision केवल Vaidya या administrator करे। Automation और operations staff clinical decision न लें।

### 6. Support/Vaidya WhatsApp workflow

1. **WhatsApp** खोलें।
2. Customer name या phone से search करें।
3. सही conversation चुनकर folio/order context जांचें।
4. Latest messages, intent, delivery status और error पढ़ें। जरूरत पर **Older messages** चुनें।
5. **HUMAN SUPPORT** दिखे तो reply से पहले पूरी बातचीत पढ़ें।
6. **Human reply** में साफ message लिखकर **Queue through WhatsApp** एक बार दबाएं।
7. Background worker message भेजता है। Status sent, delivered, read या failed हुआ या नहीं, जांचें; button बार-बार न दबाएं।
8. Delivery fail हो तो messaging escalation procedure अपनाएं। Customer की health/payment जानकारी personal messaging channel में copy न करें।

Support staff approved service information दे सकता है, लेकिन recommendation, medicine, symptom, pregnancy, allergy या urgent safety के सवाल Vaidya को भेजे।

### 7. Recommendation के बाद order बनाएं

Order केवल current approved recommendation से बन सकता है।

1. Approved recommendation वाली consultation खोलें।
2. Order section में subtotal, approved discount, tax, shipping charge और shipping address भरें।
3. Commercial details दोबारा जांचें।
4. **Create order for customer acceptance** एक बार दबाएं।
5. System एक order बनाकर customer का secure acceptance link queue करता है।

Customer का उत्तर देर से आए तो नया order न बनाएं। पहले **Orders** में existing order search करें।

### 8. Acceptance और payment

**Orders** में order status और payment status दोनों देखें।

- **Awaiting acceptance:** customer ने order accept नहीं किया।
- **Awaiting payment / Payment pending:** acceptance record हो गया, payment confirm नहीं हुआ।
- **Payment review required:** provider event को authorized जांच चाहिए।
- **Paid:** fulfillment शुरू हो सकता है।
- **Failed/Cancelled/Refunded:** approved process से नया valid paid state आए बिना fulfillment न करें।

केवल administrator **Reconcile paid** इस्तेमाल करे, वह भी provider/bank evidence स्वतंत्र रूप से verify करने के बाद। केवल order unblock करने के लिए manual reconciliation न करें।

### 9. Inventory workflow

**Item जोड़ें या update करें**

1. **Inventory** खोलें।
2. SKU, name, canonical unit (`g`, `kg`, `ml`, `l` या `unit`) और reorder level भरें।
3. **Save item** दबाएं।

एक असली item के लिए एक SKU रखें। Stock transaction होने के बाद unit को मनमाने ढंग से नहीं बदल सकते।

**Stock receive या correct करें**

1. Item SKU/public ID भरें।
2. सच में मिला stock दर्ज करने के लिए **Receipt (+)** चुनें।
3. Physical check के आधार पर approved correction के लिए ही **Adjustment (+/−)** चुनें।
4. Quantity, reference और उपयोगी non-sensitive note भरें।
5. **Post immutable transaction** दबाएं।

Transaction edit/delete नहीं हो सकता। गलती सुधारने के लिए explanation के साथ नया opposite/adjusting transaction करें।

**Standard product mapping**

1. **Finished-goods mapping** में product SKU/public ID भरें।
2. Inventory item SKU/public ID भरें।
3. एक ordered unit के लिए consume होने वाली quantity भरें।
4. Mapping save करें।

Mapping और पर्याप्त stock के बिना paid standard-product order allocate नहीं होगा।

**Formula ingredients manage करें**

1. पहले raw-material inventory item बनाएं और उसका canonical unit जांचें।
2. **Formula ingredient catalogue** में inventory item चुनें और जरूरत पर display name भरें।
3. **Add/update ingredient** दबाएं। Recommendation dropdown में केवल active catalogue entries दिखती हैं।
4. नई recommendation में selection रोकने के लिए entry deactivate करें। पुराना formula history नहीं बदलेगा।

### 10. Standard-product order पूरा करें

1. Order status **PAID** होना जांचें।
2. Product-inventory mapping और available stock जांचें।
3. **Orders** में **Allocate stock → dispatch** एक बार दबाएं।
4. System stock issue record करके order को **READY TO DISPATCH** करता है।

Stock कम या mapping missing हो तो असली stock/mapping समस्या ठीक करें। Check bypass करने के लिए गलत adjustment न डालें।

### 11. Personalized order पूरा करें

1. Personalized order का status **PAID** होना जांचें।
2. **Prepare batch** दबाएं या **Batches** खोलें।
3. Paid order public ID और approved wastage percentage भरें।
4. **Create order-sized requirements** दबाएं।
5. Approved formula और physical preparation record से हर ingredient, quantity और unit मिलाएं।
6. तैयारी सच में पूरी होने पर **Complete and consume stock** एक बार दबाएं।
7. System जरूरी stock consume करके order को dispatch के लिए ready करता है।

Ingredient missing/unmapped हो, unit अलग हो या stock कम हो तो रुकें और असली कारण ठीक करें। Vaidya-approved formula revision के बिना ingredient substitute न करें।

### 12. Dispatch और delivery

1. **Dispatch** खोलकर order खोजें।
2. **READY TO DISPATCH** order में courier और AWB/tracking number भरें।
3. Package/details जांचकर **Mark dispatched** दबाएं।
4. भरोसेमंद delivery confirmation के बाद ही **Mark delivered** दबाएं।

Delivery mark होने पर पूरी recommendation duration के आधार पर refill schedule बनता है। गलत delivery action हो जाए तो escalate करें; direct database edit से history न बदलें।

### 13. Refill workflow

**Refills** में due date, last reminder, current status और source order देखें।

- Customer वही plan चाहता है और नया review जरूरी नहीं है, तभी **Same** चुनें। इससे अधिकतम एक next order बनता है।
- Symptoms, preference, safety context, medicine या plan बदलना हो तो **Modify** चुनें।
- Customer आगे नहीं चाहता तो **Stop** चुनें।

Decision से पहले conversation पढ़ें। नया safety concern हो तो **Same** की जगह Vaidya review कराएं।

### 14. Administrator privacy workflow

केवल administrator **Privacy** screen इस्तेमाल करे।

1. Approved procedure से requester की identity form के बाहर verify करें।
2. **Privacy** खोलकर customer public ID या exact phone भरें।
3. `ACCESS`, `CORRECTION` या `ANONYMIZATION` चुनें।
4. Identity verification checkbox tick करें।
5. केवल छोटा non-sensitive verification note लिखें।
6. **Record request** दबाएं।

इसके बाद:

- **ACCESS:** Verified requester के लिए **Export** करें और approved secure तरीके से दें।
- **CORRECTION:** केवल सही किए जाने वाले fields भरकर request complete करें।
- **ANONYMIZATION:** Approval और परिणाम समझकर ही पूरा करें। इसे सामान्य reversible edit न समझें।
- **Apply legal hold:** केवल approved reason होने पर लगाएं। Hold रहने तक anonymization और automatic deletion रुकते हैं।
- **Release legal hold:** Authorization मिलने पर reason के साथ हटाएं।

Verification note में identity document, password, medical detail या payment credential न लिखें।

### 15. Staff errors और उनका सुरक्षित समाधान

| Message/स्थिति | मतलब और क्या करें |
|---|---|
| **401 / Authentication required** | फिर login करें। Session expire या revoke हो सकता है। |
| **403 / No permission** | Role अनुमति नहीं देता, password rotation बाकी है, या request approved page से नहीं आई। दूसरे का account इस्तेमाल न करें। |
| **409 / State conflict** | Record किसी और ने/process ने बदल दिया। Page reload करें, current status पढ़ें, फिर निर्णय लें। |
| **422 / Validation error** | Field missing/invalid/बहुत लंबा है या value/unit supported नहीं है। Input ठीक करें। |
| Action button नहीं दिख रहा | Current role या record status उस action की अनुमति नहीं देता। Prerequisites देखें। |
| Insufficient stock | Physical stock, receipt/adjustment, mapping, unit, order quantity और wastage जांचें। |
| WhatsApp pending | Outbox worker ने अभी message नहीं भेजा। Status देखें; देर होने पर escalate करें। |
| WhatsApp failed | Error पढ़कर messaging incident process अपनाएं। Message बार-बार duplicate न करें। |
| Anonymization blocked | Legal hold जांचें और authorized direction लें। |
| Login locked | 15 मिनट इंतज़ार करें और जरूरत पर account support प्रक्रिया अपनाएं। |

### 16. Staff के लिए जरूरी बातें

**यह करें**

- हर action से पहले customer और public record number verify करें।
- केवल पहली concern नहीं, सभी concerns और safety flags पढ़ें।
- Role boundaries और approvals का पालन करें।
- Notes में factual और कम से कम जरूरी जानकारी लिखें।
- Conflict आने पर record reload करें।
- Logout करें और अनजान sessions revoke करें।

**यह न करें**

- Account, password या provider secret share न करें।
- Payment, stock, safety या workflow checks bypass न करें।
- असली evidence के बिना payment, production, dispatch या delivery mark न करें।
- Operational गलती ठीक करने के लिए direct database edit न करें।
- Notes में अनावश्यक personal, medical या payment data न डालें।
- Medical result का वादा न करें और automation से recommendation approve न कराएं।
